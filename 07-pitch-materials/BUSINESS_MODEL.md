# CovA — Business Model & Stakeholder P&L

CovA operates on a B2B2C model, integrated directly into gig delivery platforms (Zepto, Blinkit, Swiggy) as a Guidewire-native embedded insurance module. By converting volatile income loss into a quantifiable, insurable risk, CovA creates a sustainable revenue model that benefits all stakeholders across the delivery ecosystem.

---

## 1. Unit Economics (Per Worker / Week)

**Revenue Model:**
- **Gross Premium:** ₹35 per week per worker (average).
- **Collection Method:** Micro-deductions handled natively within the platform wallet (e.g., Zepto Partner app) during weekly settlements.

**Projected Payouts (Loss Ratio estimation):**
- Expected claim frequency: 1 claim every 6-8 weeks (severe weather/disruption).
- Average payout: ₹400 (4 hours of disrupted work).
- **Annualized Premium:** ~₹1,820
- **Annualized Payout Base:** ~₹1,200
- **Target Loss Ratio:** 65%

---

## 2. Detailed Stakeholder P&L

CovA is designed to be a win-win-win. The cost of the premium is offset by the operational savings and retention benefits provided to the delivery platform, while the worker gains material financial stability.

### The Delivery Platform (e.g., Zepto, Blinkit)
*The platform acts as the distributor and data provider, integrating the CovA module via APIs.*

| P&L Driver | Impact | Estimated Value (Per Worker/Month) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Costs** | Tech Integration & minor API overhead | -₹5 |
| **Costs** | Premium co-pay (Optional employer subsidy) | -₹70 |
| **Savings/Revenue** | Elimination of ad-hoc "Surge Incentives" required to keep workers active in bad weather | +₹150 |
| **Savings/Revenue** | Reduction in churn / acquisition costs (Lower turnover due to income stability) | +₹200 |
| **Net Impact** | **Net Positive ROI** | **+₹275** |

*Value Prop:* Platforms save millions historically lost to uncontrolled surge pricing and high worker churn rates.

### The Delivery Worker (Gig Partner)
*The insured party.*

| P&L Driver | Impact | Estimated Value (Per Worker/Month) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Costs** | Premium deduction (50% co-pay) | -₹70 |
| **Revenue/Protection** | Parametric payout during weather disruption (₹400 / incident, avg 0.5 incidents/mo) | +₹200 |
| **Savings** | Elimination of uncompensated idle time (gas, transit to dead zones) | +₹100 |
| **Net Impact** | **Income Stability** | **+₹230** |

*Value Prop:* Material financial protection against climate risk, ensuring they can put food on the table even when it rains.

### The Insurer (Powered by Guidewire)
*The underwriter.*

| P&L Driver | Impact | Estimated Value (Per Worker/Month) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Revenue** | Gross Premium Collected | +₹140 |
| **Costs** | Expected Claim Payouts (Loss Ratio: ~65%) | -₹90 |
| **Costs** | Loss Adjustment Expenses (LAE) | -₹2 (Zero-touch processing!) |
| **Net Impact** | **Operating Profit (Combined Ratio ~68%)** | **+₹48** |

*Value Prop:* Near-zero Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) via platform distribution, and zero Loss Adjustment Expenses (LAE) due to parametric Oracle triggers.

## 3. Scale Potential (India Market)
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** 8 Million active gig workers in India.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** Targeting 150,000 workers in Phase 1 (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi).
- **Phase 1 ARR Target:** ~₹27 Crores ($3.2M)
- **Moat:** Multi-platform UWID layer prevents overlapping policies and allows risk scoring to travel with the worker.
